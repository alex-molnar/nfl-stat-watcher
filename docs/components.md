# UI components

## Header (pinned)
The header is pinned to the top of every page (Players, Vs Mode, Leagues, Settings), on screens wider than 760px and taller than 30em. A phone wraps it to several rows and a very short window would lose most of its height to it (WCAG 1.4.10), so there it scrolls away like any content. `Header` publishes its measured height as `--header-h`; the Vs score bar sticks at that offset directly under it, the settings left menu likewise, and `scroll-padding-top` includes it (and the score bar on Vs), so a focused card is never hidden behind either.

Props: `actions?: ReactNode` (extra buttons, for example "Add player").
Shows the football icon (inline, the same drawing as the tab icon) and the app name on the left, then the page's own `actions`, then the Players, Vs Mode, Leagues and Settings links (current page marked with `aria-current`) and the theme toggle pinned to the right edge (`.top-end`), so the tabs and toggle stay at the same position on every page however many buttons a page adds.

## ThemeToggle
No props. A switch with a sun on the left and a crescent on the right and a thumb that slides over the active theme. It has no visible text; its accessible name is "Dark mode" in the light theme and "Light mode" in the dark theme. The name names the action and follows the effective theme (stored choice, else the OS preference, including OS changes while open), so it never goes stale. Stores the choice in `nflsw:v1:theme`.

## Mascot and MascotSays
`Mascot` (`src/ui/Mascot.tsx`, props `size` in px, default 160, and `className`) is the Stat Watch mascot: the Gridiron design chosen in the design notes (`docs/mascot.md`), a football in glasses, in striped socks, cleats and mint gloves. It is decorative (`aria-hidden`), so whatever it "says" is real text beside it. Its eyes follow the pointer (a window `pointermove` listener sets `--lx` and `--ly` on the svg, which the CSS turns into a small shift of the pupils) and it blinks, glances and bobs by itself. All motion is CSS transform and opacity (`.mascot` rules in `styles.css`) and all of it stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`; pointer-following is skipped then too. Each instance has its own gradient and clip ids (`useId`). The moving parts are classes (`m-root`, `m-look`, `m-glance`, `m-lid`, `m-pupil`, `m-brow`, and the limbs `m-leg-l`, `m-leg-r`, `m-arm-l`, `m-arm-r`, each turning about its own joint), so walking and pointing can be added later without redrawing it. `MascotSays` (props `text`, `pointAt`, `minLines`, and children such as a button) puts the mascot beside a speech bubble. The jump into the place (see below) is part of arriving on the page, so the bubble waits for it: while a jump is under way (`isMascotFlying`, `subscribeMascotFlight`) the bubble is hidden (`.waiting`, at once, so it never shows under the mascot), then it fades in as the mascot lands, and the text starts typing 120 ms later; if a jump begins after the text had started, the text is cleared and starts over after the landing. Its text types itself out (`TypedText`, `src/ui/TypedText.tsx`): about 28 ms per letter, never more than about 1.5 s for any text, the first text starting 350 ms after the page so the entrance finishes first, and a replacement text (a hover hint) starting at once. Screen readers get the whole sentence at once from a visually hidden copy in a polite live region and never the letters one by one; an invisible copy of the whole text holds the space so nothing moves as the letters arrive; under reduced motion the text is simply there. `MascotSays` puts the mascot beside a speech bubble (`.bubble`, with a tail pointing at it; stacked, tail up, on narrow screens). `pointAt="left"` lifts the arm on that side after the entrance (420 ms, held) and fixes the eyes on it instead of the pointer; on narrow screens, where the menu is above, it points up. It is used for every empty state: Players with no league (with a "Go to Leagues" link) and with leagues but nobody followed yet (with the Add player button in the bubble; when a league is imported the text adds "Alternatively sync your starters from your imported leagues." and a Sync starters button sits next to Add player, in `.bubble-actions`), the Vs screen without a league, and Leagues with no league or with leagues but none selected (the bubble then says "Select a league from the menu to edit its scoring, or add or import one.", and the hover hints work the same); on Leagues it points at the menu and the bubble says "You have no leagues yet. Add or import one from the menu to start following players." While the mascot is pointing, hovering or focusing a menu button changes the bubble to explain it (Add a league: presets or full customizing; Import leagues: ESPN only, rules synced automatically; Import StatWatch profile: bring leagues and players from another browser or device), and leaving it brings the greeting back. The bubble text is a polite live region and keeps room for the longest hint so the panel does not jump.

**In the header:** the old football icon is gone. The mascot is to the right of the "Stat Watch" title and has no entrance of its own (`entrance={false}`), because the header is rebuilt on every page. On a screen at least 1000 px wide it is 82 px and **sits on the bottom edge of the header with its legs hanging over it** (`seated`): the header is the containing block (`.top-bar` is `position: relative`), the mascot is hung from the edge by `--hang`, the part of the drawing below the body (`SEAT_Y`), its legs are longer, it has no ground shadow and does not bob, its legs swing gently out of step, and it ignores the pointer (`pointer-events: none`) so the legs never block what they overlap. On narrower screens, where the header wraps, it stands beside the title at 52 px, without the fine detail (`.mascot.sm` hides the `detail` parts at 48 px and below). **While a drill waits, the page is dead except for what the drill needs:** a capture-phase guard in `RookieCamp` swallows presses (pointer, mouse, click, and Enter or Space) on everything but the camp card, the control the drill points at (or the page's tab, while the user is elsewhere) and the drill's `also` control (Import leagues for the league drill, `data-camp="import-leagues"`). It stands down while a dialog is open, so a dialog opened from the target is fully usable. While Rookie camp has the mascot, an empty state (`MascotSays`) shows its sentence and buttons as plain text, the same as with the mascot off, and talks again when the camp ends (`campOpen` in `dialogsOpen.ts`). It steps aside when the page shows a mascot of its own: the page passes `pageMascot` to its `Header` (Players and Leagues with no league, and the Vs screen without one). The page knows this while it renders; a header that waited to be told by the page's mascot would show its own for a moment first and then jump off, and a mascot leaving and another arriving in two steps made two jumps in opposite directions.

**It never disappears: the jump** (`src/ui/mascotFlight.ts`). When a mascot leaves one place and another appears in a new one at the same moment, a copy of it jumps from the first to the second: off the header's edge down to an empty state ("jumped off"), or from an empty state up onto the edge ("jumps back up"), and between the empty states of two pages. Leaving and arriving are separate components, so the two are matched up: `Mascot` reports both in a layout effect (`mascotAppeared`, `mascotLeft`; where it was is kept up to date on every render, scroll and resize, because it cannot be measured when it leaves: React has by then removed the rest of the old page, the header for one, and everything has shifted up, which made the jump start from the wrong place and look like a teleport), whichever comes first waits up to 200 ms for the other, and the copy first crouches for 260 ms where it was (the legs splay out and shorten, the body dips in a squash and the arms are flung up and flail), then flies with the Web Animations API (transform only), the arms flailing and the legs tucked in the air, the body stretching on take-off, and every limb back at rest before it lands: up fast and slowing, then falling faster, the arc kept on screen, 480 to 900 ms depending on the distance, scaled between the two sizes. The real mascot waits hidden (`in-flight`, its own animations paused so that, for example, the pointing arm lifts after it lands) and, on landing, gets a small squash (`landed`, a class it then keeps: taking it off would hand the mascot back to its entrance animation, which would play again as a flicker). Nothing happens when it did not really move (the header is rebuilt on every page), when the two are more than 200 ms apart, under `prefers-reduced-motion`, or where the browser has no Web Animations API; in all of these the mascot simply appears.

**In dialogs** (`DialogMascot`, `src/ui/DialogMascot.tsx`, `dialogsOpen.ts`). Every dialog (add player, import leagues, sync starters, highlights, export and import profile, delete league, clear data) has a `<DialogMascot />` as its first child. The browser draws a modal dialog in its top layer, above the page and its backdrop, so the dialog has to hold the mascot itself. It **sits on the dialog's top edge near the left corner**, seated like in the header (72 px, always, with its legs hanging into the dialog): since it hangs from the corner, the dialog's size, which changes constantly, does not matter. For that the dialog no longer clips (`overflow: visible`) and its content (`.dlg`) scrolls instead, with room above and below (`max-height: calc(100dvh - 11rem)`); the highlights dialog's own scrolling was removed for the same reason, and nothing else about it is special. The dialog's own `open` attribute is watched (a `MutationObserver`), so it needs no props; the mascot appears once the dialog's opening animation (190 ms) has settled, and goes when it closes. **It is the same single mascot:** from the moment it is in the dialog the page's mascots (the header's, an empty state's, which keeps its space) step aside (`registerOpenDialog`), so it jumps from the page into the dialog and back when the dialog closes, and there is never a moment without one. The jumping copy is a popover (`.mascot-flight`, `showPopover()`), which is drawn in the top layer after the dialog, so it flies over the backdrop. **Hints:** a control with `data-hint` (put on it with `useHint()`: `{...hint('text')}`) makes a bubble beside the mascot, above the dialog's top edge, type that text while the control is hovered or focused; a live region is always there so it is announced once, whole. Hints are on the search field and league select (add player), the league links, season and Load leagues (import leagues), the team select and the remove-non-starters box (sync starters), the paste box and override box (import profile), copy and Download (export profile), the delete option and Delete league button (delete league) and both buttons of the clear data dialog. With the mascot off in Settings nothing is added to a dialog and `useHint()` gives the control a `title` tooltip with the same text. **Worried:** a dialog about something that cannot be undone has the attribute `data-worried`, and its mascot then looks worried (`worried` on `Mascot`): the brows slant up in the middle (`.m-brow-l` and `.m-brow-r` turn about their own centres by 17 degrees and lift 3 px) and the smile turns into a frown (`.m-mouth` flipped), easing in over 220 ms. That is the Delete league and Clear my data dialogs always, and the Import profile dialog only while "Override existing profiles" is ticked (the attribute comes and goes while the dialog is open, and the face follows).

## RookieCamp
`src/ui/RookieCamp.tsx` (the engine), no props, mounted once in `AppRoutes` (so it follows the user from page to page); the drills are data in `src/ui/campDrills.ts`; state in `campStore` (`src/storage/camp.ts`, key `nflsw:v1:camp`: `{ phase: 'idle' | 'running' | 'finished' | 'done' | 'declined', step, sub }`: the drill and the step inside it; a state saved without `sub` starts the drill from its first step, an invalid one falls back to the default). A guided first practice of **drills, each a list of steps done by the user on the real page**. Now: (1) a league, (2) following a player, (3) Vs Mode, (4) importing a league, (5) syncing starters, (6) a video highlight (the last, which ends in the finish dialog); more drills and steps are added in `campDrills.ts`. Nothing is passed over because the state already satisfies it (a user with a league still does drill 1's steps, and a practice league still leaves drill 2 to do): progress is the user doing the steps, or pressing Skip drill.
- **Steps (`campDrills.ts`).** A `Drill` has `on` (the page its controls are on; elsewhere the camp points at that page's tab and says `go`; a `Step` may have its own `on` and `go`, as Vs Mode's steps do), `skipIf(facts)` (the whole drill is passed over at once: Vs Mode with no league, syncing starters with no ESPN league, since the practice league has none), `practice` (offers the practice league on its first step) and `steps`. A stored place the list no longer has (an old step index, a `sub` past the drill's end) lands on the start of the drill, or on the finish. A `Step` has the `text` Fumble says; `modal` (not a ring and a card but the dialog `CampTour`, see below); `wait` (its controls come and go, such as a result that is loading or a box that shows only sometimes: while none is on the page the card stays out of sight, the mascot staying in the dialog, and after 4 s it appears anyway with Skip drill and Leave camp, so the user is never stuck behind an invisible card); `target`: selectors, **one ring around the union of those that are on the page** (and they are what can be pressed); `also`: more that stays pressable; `backdrop`: a click on the area around a dialog counts (the step about closing it); `needs`/`optional`: what must be on the page for the step to make sense, if not its first target (gone for 700 ms the camp steps **back** to the step that brings it up, or **on** for an `optional` step); and how it is done: `next` (a button on the card with that label, before Skip drill and Leave camp; on a drill's last step it reads **Complete drill**), `press` (a click on this; with `dialog` only once a dialog really opened from it), `when(ctx)` (done when it turns true, looked at five times a second) or `skipIf(ctx)` (passed over at once). `ctx` is `{ facts, q, memo }`: the state (leagues, followed, path), a lookup of what is on the page (`q`, which ignores a closed dialog's contents) and a note that lasts for the step (`changed(selector)` uses it: true once a field is not what it was when the step began). They combine: a step may have `next` and a `press` or `when`, so the user is never forced to do what is suggested (Apply preset is disabled until another preset is picked, so it has Next too). Controls the camp points at carry a `data-camp` attribute where a class would be brittle (`add-league`, `league-name`, `league-color`, `league-preset`, `league-apply`, `add-player`, `add-dialog-search`, `add-dialog-league`, `add-dialog-results`; the Add buttons of the search results carry `data-result` with the player's name). **Skip drill** is `skipDrill(step)`: it closes every open dialog (`dialog.close()` plus a synchronous `close` event, so an owner that keeps its dialog open by state hears of it before it re-renders and reopens it; a modal dialog would make the next drill's tab unpressable) and moves on; skipping drill 1 first chooses the practice league. **Leave camp** (`leaveCamp`) closes them the same way (one helper, `closeOpenDialogs`) and then ends the camp, so the mascot goes back to the page in the same moment.
- **Drill 1, a league.** Off the Leagues page the card points at the Leagues tab (only Skip drill and Leave camp). On it: only **Add a league** is ringed and pressable (Import leagues is for a later drill), with "Alternatively use a practice league, to get things going." and the green **Use a practice league** button, which ends the drill (not drill 2). Pressing Add a league shows the editor, and the steps go through it: name (Next), color (Next), the preset list (done when another preset is picked, or Next), Apply preset (done when pressed, or Next, since it is disabled until another preset is picked), the first setting (Next: a preset is only a start), a rule's checkbox (Next: any rule can be switched off), and Save (done when the Save button is pressed; Save is a new league's draft until then).
- **Drill 3, Vs Mode.** The tab is ringed (done when Vs Mode is open); then `CampTour` (`src/ui/CampTour.tsx`, mounted next to the others): a modal dialog like the welcome and the finish, where the mascot jumps up onto its edge and says what the page is, with Next, Skip drill and Leave camp (Escape counts as Next; there is no backdrop answer). It is on the page it is about; elsewhere the camp points at the tab again. Then the rings: the league picker (what it is, and its options: one league, or All), the score bar (what it is, and that how accurate it is depends on the league's settings, so the real score at the league's own site is the one to trust), the Add player on the user's side (the same as the one already learned) and the Add player on the opponent's side, with **Complete drill** (those players show only on this page). Each has Next. A drill is passed over without a league.
- **Drill 4, importing a league.** On the Leagues page the Import leagues button is ringed (done when its dialog has the mascot); in the dialog the link box and the season are ringed together (where to find the league's link or number; done when the link box is clicked, or focus comes back to it after it was elsewhere, but not the season); Load leagues (done when results are shown; the fields stay pressable); **if the league is private** the whole box that asks for the settings to be pasted is ringed and the step waits for the paste (a public league's result passes it over); the lineup slots and scoring that were found (Next); **if there are warnings** them (Next); and their checkbox (done when ticked: accepting them is the only way to import a league with approximate rules, today); and Import selected leagues, which finishes the drill (done when pressed; the button is disabled until the warnings are accepted).
- **Drill 5, syncing starters.** Passed over without an ESPN league. On the Players page the header's Sync starters is ringed (done when its dialog has the mascot); **if the league is private** the box for pasted rosters (passed over once the team list is there); the "choose your team" select (done when a team is chosen); the "remove every non starter player" checkbox (Next, so the user is not forced to tick it); the added / removed / unchanged lists in one go (Next); and the dialog's Sync starters button, which finishes the drill. `data-camp` hooks: `import-links`, `import-season`, `import-load`, `import-commit`, `sync-starters`, `starters-team`, `starters-remove`, `starters-sync`, `vs-add-mine`, `vs-add-opponent`.
- **Drill 6, the cards (practice players).** The last drill shows three practice players, and **only** those: while it runs (and until the congratulation is dismissed) the Players page filters its list to them (`showsDummiesOnly` in `storage/campLeague.ts`; nothing stored changes, and everything is back when the camp ends), so every kind of card is there at any time of year and the highlight drill can always be finished: **Cole Harlan** (QB, playing now: live points, the mini field, and a red zone flag once the offense is inside the 20), **Marcus Teller** (RB, not started: kickoff time and "No stats until kickoff") and **Jalen Whitmore** (WR, final: the result, and a ▶ Highlights button with one clip). They are invented, on invented teams (ids start with `camp-`, so none is a real id). Steps: three Next steps ring each card in turn (its header and stats, not its footer, so Remove and the league select stay dead; optional, so a card that is gone is passed over), then the highlight button is ringed and done by pressing it (a dialog must open); the highlights dialog gets the mascot and the card goes into it, and one more step rings the dialog's X (`dialog.hl-dlg[open] .close`; a click outside and Escape stay allowed) saying to press it to go back; that step is done when the dialog closes, and the congratulation, which waits for the dialog to close as before, follows. **How they exist** (`src/espn/campSandbox.ts`, `src/storage/campLeague.ts`): the ESPN client answers `camp-` ids itself (`getSummary`, `getAthlete`, `getClipAthletes`) with canned responses in ESPN's own shapes, so the normal parsing, scoring and cards run unchanged, and every real id still goes to ESPN; the scoreboard query adds the three fake games beside the real ones through `select` while the sandbox is on (never cached with the real schedule; they are not in search results). The live game advances every 10 seconds the sandbox has been on (a pass in midfield to a touchdown, then it stays). The players live in the stored **"Practice league"** (id `camp-league`, described above) that `syncCampLeague` keeps in step with the camp: present while `practice` is set, from the last drill on in any case, until the congratulation is dismissed (phase `running` with `practice` or on the last drill, or `finished`), gone at any other time. It runs a microtask after the camp's state changes (so a `reloadAllStores` has finished and no old data is written back), at startup (which clears what a tab closed mid-drill left behind), and removes the players before their league (an entry without a league would be handed to the user's first real one). Nothing of it is left in localStorage afterwards, and the user's own leagues and players are never touched. The practice clip is `public/camp-clip.mp4` (4 s, about 96 KB, an animation recorded from a canvas); a clip link from this site's own address is accepted as well as an https one (`httpsUrl` in `stats/normalize.ts`). If ESPN's schedule cannot be loaded the fake games are missing too, and the cards say "Game status unavailable".
- **Drill 2, following a player.** Add player on the Players page (done when its dialog is open); then the dialog's two fields are ringed together (search and league, with why leagues matter: each one scores differently), prompting to search "Maye" (done when the field contains it); then the Add button next to Drake Maye (the first addable result if he is not there), done when a result reads "added"; then the dialog's X (and the area around it counts too), done when the dialog is closed. **With the practice league** (`Facts.practice`; a step's `text` can be a function of the facts, `stepText`) the player is **Jalen Whitmore**, the third practice player, instead of Drake Maye: the search step says to choose the Practice league and try "Whitmore", the Add dialog opens on the Practice league (`AddDialog` defaults to `camp-league` while it exists), the search finds him (`fakeSearch` in `espn/campSandbox.ts`, put first in `searchPlayers`' results, only while the sandbox is on and the camp is on drill 2, so he never shows in search at any other time), and the Add button next to his result is ringed. Without it (the user did drill 1 with a league of their own) the old wording and a real search for Maye stay. Skipping drill 2 puts him in the practice league silently. **The nudge:** a step can have a `nudge` (`when`, `text`) and a `back`. On the search step, while the field holds something that is neither the asked name, nor a start of it ("Whit", "drake m", any case), nor a longer form of it, the card says "That is a good one, but for this drill search for Whitmore." (Maye without the practice league) and the mascot looks worried (the card's own mascot via `worried`; inside a dialog the engine sets `data-worried` on the open dialog while it applies); the normal text returns when the field is empty or fits. The Add step goes back to the search step (`back`, once) when the field no longer holds the name, and the nudge then applies.
- **Dialogs.** A modal dialog blocks everything outside it, so **while a step lives in one the card (and the ring) are rendered into the open dialog** (a portal into the top open `dialog`), the mascot is on that dialog's edge as in any dialog (the card then has none of its own), and back on the card when the dialog closes. The click guard works in dialogs too: while a step waits only its targets, `also`, the card and (for `backdrop` steps) the area around a dialog can be pressed. Escape cannot be blocked cleanly, so a dialog closed mid-drill is allowed, and the camp steps back to the step that opens it.
- **The card and the ring.** A ring (`.camp-ring`, `aria-hidden`, `pointer-events: none`, so it never takes a click; a pulse that stops under reduced motion) is drawn around what the step is about, found by selector (one ring around the union of the targets on the page, `union`; the card and the mascot use that rectangle too) and followed while the page scrolls, resizes and changes (a 250 ms look, because the control may not exist yet). The card (`.camp`, a region labelled "Rookie camp") goes to the right of a small control in the page (a menu button, so it does not cover the buttons under it), below a control in the header (with room for the mascot on its edge), or above when there is no room, kept on screen (`placeCard`), and in the bottom corner when there is nothing to point at and on a narrow screen (under 700 px, where it would cover the controls around the one it points at; only the ring marks the control then). It has the mascot's name (`mascotNameStore`) and the drill number, the text typed out in a polite live region (`TypedText`; screen readers get each step once, whole), and the buttons: **Next** when the step has one, then **Skip drill** (the whole drill) and **Leave camp** (a danger button, in red), always there. A control below the fold is scrolled into view once.
- **The mascot comes along.** The card has the mascot on its top edge near the left corner, seated like in a dialog (72 px; it reuses `.dialog-perch`). **There is only ever one:** while the camp is shown it registers with `dialogsOpen.ts` (`registerCampMascot`; `mascotAway()` is what the header's and the empty states' mascots watch, next to `dialogsOpen()`, which only the camp watches to step aside for a real dialog), so the page's mascots step aside, it jumps from the page onto the card, and back to the page when the camp ends. It also holds its place while a dialog has the mascot (it jumps to the dialog and back), or the page's would show for a moment when the dialog closes; the card itself stays, in the dialog. From drill to drill it jumps (`mascotFlight.ts`): the mascot is keyed by the selector the card was last measured for (`useTarget` returns the selector with the rectangle), so a new place is a new mascot, matched with the old one leaving, in the same commit as the card's move; the card is hidden while it flies (`.waiting`) and its text starts typing after it lands. A drill is never moved on twice at once, so two jumps are never sent together. **Faces** (`Mascot` props): `happy` for 1.8 s after a drill is done (brows lifted, wider smile; not after Skip drill), `worried` while the pointer or focus is on Skip drill or Leave camp.
- **Practice league.** One league, one set of players: the camp's own, **"Practice league"** (id `camp-league`), holding the three practice players of drill 6 (Cole Harlan, Marcus Teller, Jalen Whitmore; see drill 6 below for how they exist). It starts with two of them, Harlan and Teller; Whitmore is what the user adds in drill 2, and is added silently when drill 2 is skipped or when the last drill is reached without him (`syncCampLeague`). The first step of drill 1, once the user is on the Leagues page (not while the card points at the Leagues tab: then only Skip drill and Leave camp), has **Use a practice league** as a green button (`btn-primary`, the accent) and the text "Alternatively use a practice league, to get things going." after the usual one. The welcome dialog has none. Choosing it, or pressing **Skip drill** in any step of drill 1 (the drills after it need a league to work on), sets `practice: true` in the camp's state (`choosePractice`; `patchCamp` keeps it as the camp moves on) and moves on to drill 2, which the user still does. The league, its three players and the sandbox (fake games on every page) then exist from that moment until the camp ends; at the last drill they are made anyway if the user did drill 1 with a league of their own. It is **not an ordinary league**: it is gone when the camp is done, left or declined, and at startup if a tab was closed mid-camp; at those times every `camp-` entry is removed from every league, in case Whitmore was added to a real one. A real player the user adds to it (Drake Maye in drill 2) goes with it.
- **Across pages.** When the user is on another page than the drill's control, the ring goes round that page's tab (Leagues, Players) and the text says to open it; on the right page it points at the control itself.
- **Offer, finish, replay.** A user with no league who has not answered yet is welcomed once by `CampWelcome` (`src/ui/CampWelcome.tsx`, mounted next to `RookieCamp` in `AppRoutes`): 700 ms after the page has loaded the mascot jumps up onto the edge of a modal `dialog` in the middle of the screen (the ordinary dialog mascot, so it jumps in from the page's and there is never a frame without one or two of them), types "Hi, I’m Fumble!" (the name from Settings) and says who it is and what the camp is, and asks: and adds that the user can turn it off at any point in Settings. Three buttons: **Enter training camp** (first in the dialog, so it has the focus), **Skip**, and **Skip and disable Fumble** (the same as Skip, and it also switches the mascot off in Settings, `mascotEnabledStore`, so the app goes to plain text). The dialog is labelled by its title and described by its text, has no click-on-the-backdrop answer (a stray click should not answer for the user), and each button has a hint the mascot says from the dialog's edge (`useHint`). Entering starts the first drill: the dialog goes away because the answer is in `campStore`, the camp's mascot appears in the same commit and hops from the dialog to the first drill's control. Skip, and Escape (the dialog's `close` event, which only counts while nothing was answered), is remembered like Leave camp and the mascot jumps back to the page. The camp's card waits for its first look at its control before it shows, so the mascot does not land in the corner first and jump again. Reduced motion: the dialog simply appears, with no jump. **The finish** is a dialog too, `CampFinish` (`src/ui/CampFinish.tsx`, mounted next to the others): once the last drill is done, or skipped, a modal dialog in the middle of the screen opens with the mascot on its edge; when he has landed he hops twice (`hop`, transform only, none under reduced motion) and the title "Touchdown!" types out, with the congratulation and **Done** (focused). Done, or Escape, ends the camp for good. It waits for any other dialog to close first: when the last drill was done by opening a highlight, the congratulation comes when the user closes the highlights dialog, and the mascot jumps from the one into the other. Until it is answered it holds the page's own mascots away (`registerCampMascot`). Settings has **Start rookie camp** to take it again.

## Drive end detection

`normalizeSummary` marks the live situation `driveOver` as soon as the offense is done, instead of waiting for the next team to run a play. A drive is over when ESPN's drive `result` is set, or when the last real play is a score, kick, turnover, conversion attempt or kickoff. Timeouts, period breaks and the two minute warning are looked past, because ESPN appends them to a drive right after a touchdown or field goal. While `driveOver` is set, nobody counts as being in the red zone or on the right side of the ball, so cards lose that styling and drop in the order until the next offense starts. Turnovers on downs and some fumbles have an ordinary last play, so they only clear once ESPN sets the drive result.


## Scoring play highlight

A live card celebrates a play in one of three tiers, decided by the single `TIERS` table in `src/stats/events.ts` (move a play by editing one line):

- **Big** (touchdown, field goal, interception, fumble recovery, safety): `EntryCard` takes over the whole card. It floods from the centre in the play's colour (gold for scores, the theme accent for turnovers), the play name pops in across it at a size taken from the card width, and a touchdown throws sparks. Touchdowns and field goals last about 2.6 seconds, the other big plays 1.8. It then clears to a small tag on the card's top edge.
- **Small** (extra point, 2-point conversion, sack, blocked kick, a single 10+ yard run or catch, a single 20+ yard pass): one ring from the card edge and a tag on the corner, with the label ("Sack", "14-yard catch"). Gold for kicks, the accent for defense, blue for long gains.
- **None**: shorter gains, tackles and anything else.

Every play also has a tone, `good` or `bad`, and bad plays use the same two tiers in one red palette (the `tone-bad` class; no sparks):

- **Big, bad**: an interception thrown, a fumble lost, a missed field goal, a touchdown allowed (a defense's points allowed jumping by six or more).
- **Small, bad**: being sacked, a missed extra point, a field goal allowed (points allowed rising by exactly three).
- **None**: a point after a touchdown, a safety against, and the rest.

Fumbles lost and interceptions thrown are read for offensive players only. A long gain counts only when exactly one carry, catch or completion arrived in the refresh, so several plays bundled into one refresh are never reported as one big play. A big play wins over a small one in the same refresh. The tag goes after about four seconds. Nothing loops, and a screen reader hears "<name>: <play>" through a polite live region on the card.

`scoringEvent` (`src/stats/events.ts`) compares two refreshes of the same game and reports the highest tier play whose count grew or whose single-play gain met the threshold. `useCelebration` calls it when new data arrives for a live game. The first data a card sees is only a baseline, so a reload never replays old plays, and games that are not live never animate. With reduced motion there is no sweep, pop, sparks or ring: the colour and the word simply appear and fade out, and small plays show just the tag. In forced colors the overlay uses system colors and a highlight border.


## Injury designations

Designations come from two ESPN sources. The game summary carries an injury report per team, but ESPN cuts it to five players per team, so most injured players are missing from it. The league-wide injury report (`/nfl/injuries`, about 350 KB over the wire) is complete, so `useLeagueInjuries` fetches it once and refreshes it every five minutes (not while paused); `parseLeagueInjuries` keys it by athlete id (taken from the player card link) and drops "Active" players. A card uses the game's own entry when it has one, because that updates every ten seconds during a game, and the league report otherwise (`injuryOf`), so a player out before kickoff or ruled out mid-game still shows. The summary's report is also kept as `GameStats.injuries`, keyed by athlete id. Games that have not started are now fetched too (one summary per game, refreshed every ten minutes, stopped by Pause), because designations change before kickoff.

`EntryCard` shows the designation as a chip beside the name ("Out · Ankle", "Questionable · Hamstring"); the word carries the meaning and the colour reinforces it (red for out, orange for doubtful, amber for questionable, neutral otherwise). A player ruled out (`isOut` in `src/stats/injury.ts`: out, injured reserve, suspension, PUP) is ranked last in the Live now group whatever the ball is doing, even before the game situation is known, and never gets the on-field or red zone styling. Questionable and doubtful players keep their normal order. Byes and games ESPN has no report for show no chip.


## Order during and after a celebration

When a refresh shows a celebrated play by a card's own player or defense (`scoringEvent`, any tier), `useLiveOrder` does two things to that card only (`RankHolds` in `src/stats/liveOrder.ts`; every other card moves at once):

- **Hold:** for as long as the celebration lasts (`SHOW_MS`, about four seconds) the card never sits lower than the rank it had before the play. After a touchdown the rest of the offense and the opposing defense slide back immediately while the scorer's card stays put under its animation. A hold only stops a card sliding down; it never delays one moving up.
- **Boost:** for `BOOST_MS` (30 seconds) the card sits at the top of its own group (red zone, on the field, or the rest), keeping the configured position order among boosted cards, but never past the group above it. A player with a celebrated long gain outside the red zone rises above unboosted cards in that activity group, and stays below everyone in the red zone. Another play extends the boost. Unranked and ruled-out cards are not boosted. A big bad play (an interception thrown, a fumble lost, a touchdown allowed, a missed field goal) only holds: the card stays put under its red animation, since the possession change moves it down, and then moves down by the normal rules with no lift. Small bad plays (sacked, a missed extra point, a field goal allowed) lift the card like any small play.

A second play during a hold keeps the original held rank and extends it. The hook sets a timer for the next hold or boost end so the page re-sorts then.

On cards at least 400px wide it reads "Highlights 2"; on narrower ones just "▶ 2", so it fits beside Remove. A dot marks clips not opened yet during this page visit (kept in memory only, so a reload shows them as new again). Team defenses have no player tag and show no button.

Clips come from the `videos` list in the game summary the card already polls (every ten seconds while live), so a new clip appears within about ten seconds of ESPN publishing it, with no extra request. The summary lists clips without player tags, so each clip's tags are fetched once from ESPN's per-clip API (`getClipAthletes`) and cached for the session, shared by every card. Clips are shown newest first.

`HighlightsDialog` is a native `<dialog>`. A clip with a direct `.mp4` file plays inside it (a `video` element with controls); a single clip starts straight away. A clip with only a page opens that page in a new window (`noopener,noreferrer`). Only https links are accepted, and only `.mp4` files are played in the dialog.

The source is ESPN, not `api.nfl.com/content/v1/videos`: that endpoint answers 401 (`x-nfl-jwtstatus: FAILED`) to any request without an NFL-issued token, and a token sent from the browser would be visible to every user.


## Closing dialogs from the backdrop

Every dialog (add player, import leagues, import starters, highlights) also closes when the dimmed area around it is clicked, through the shared `backdropClose` props in `src/ui/backdropClose.ts`. The dialog fills its own box, so a click whose target is the `<dialog>` element itself landed on the backdrop. The press must also have started there, so selecting text inside a dialog and releasing the mouse outside it does not close it. The native `close` event still runs each dialog's own cleanup (aborting a running import, stopping the video, restoring focus).


## Card header layout

The header is a three-column grid. Row 1: the name, as wide as it can go (wrapping only when it must), and the points button, which also spans the row below. Rows 2 and 3: the full-size team circle with the three-letter code, spanning both rows, beside the team, position and opponent line and, under it, the league label. An injury designation is its own optional row beneath (absent for healthy players). Compact (non-live) cards use less padding (12px 14px), and cards narrower than 340px a smaller name and statistic type, so names and stat lines fit on one row more often. The bottom row (highlights, league, Remove) is pinned to the card's bottom edge, so in a row of cards of different heights every Remove button lines up. Compact cards (everything except Live now) also have a minimum height of 266px, the height of the longest possible one (two-line name, injury row, stat line and bottom row, measured on Antoine Winfield Jr.), so rows look even and shorter cards just have empty space above the bottom row. A card with more content (a wrapped stat line, the open points breakdown) still grows past it.

## League colour

Every profile has a colour (`Profile.color`, `#rrggbb`). A new league, made with Add a league or by importing, gets the next unused colour from `LEAGUE_COLORS` (`src/scoring/leagueColor.ts`; once all ten are used it starts over from the least used). Profiles saved before colours existed are given different ones in list order the first time they load. Leagues has a Color field (a native colour input) beside the name with a live preview tag, and the profile list shows the colour as a dot. The league tag on every card uses the colour as its background, with black or white text chosen for contrast (`textOn`). Refreshing a league from ESPN keeps its colour.


## Private league lineups bookmark

`PrivateLeagueHelp` can offer a bookmarklet (`bookmarklet` prop) beside the manual copy steps. It is a draggable `a` whose `javascript:` href is set on the element (React refuses that URL in a prop); clicking it on this site only explains to drag it. The code (`rosterBookmarklet`) is generated for one league and season, validates both as plain numbers, and writes a trimmed copy of ESPN's roster response in ESPN's own shape to the clipboard, so the same parser (`readLineups`) reads it.

The box is a paste target: a `paste` event takes the clipboard text, replaces the box's content with it and runs the import at once, showing any problem (`role="alert"`) under the box and leaving the pasted text visible. Text typed or dropped in instead is imported with the "Import these ..." button, which stays disabled while the box is empty. This applies to both the roster and the settings copy flows.


## Leagues page: selecting, editing and saving

- **No league is selected** when the page opens. The right side says to pick one (or add or import one) and shows no form.
- **Header and left menu stay in view.** The "Leagues" title is the first thing in the left menu and sticks with it, just below the pinned header (see Header).
- **Left menu** (`.settings-side`, sticky above the 760px breakpoint so it stays in view while the long form scrolls): the league list, Add a league, Import leagues, then, once a league is selected, **Delete profile** (the same danger button and the same move-the-cards confirmation as before, moved here from the bottom of the form), then, while there are unsaved changes, **Save** and **Cancel**.
- **Edits go to a working copy**, not to storage. Changing the name, colour, preset, any value, switch, step or points-allowed tier shows Save and Cancel in the left menu and again at the end of the form, with an "Unsaved changes" note. **Save** writes the name, colour, preset and values to the saved profile (a name that is empty or clashes with another league is corrected the same way as on blur) and says "Saved <name>."; **Cancel** puts the saved values back, including in the name box. Apply preset also only changes the working copy.
- **Leaving with unsaved changes** (choosing another league or adding one) asks "Discard the unsaved changes to <name>?"; closing or reloading the tab triggers the browser's own warning. The working copy starts over when the saved league changes underneath it (an import, a refresh), so it never shows stale values.
- Not edits, so they happen at once: Delete league after its dialog, Refresh settings and Disconnect source (ESPN source section), and the saved team and removal choice of Sync starters.
- The pure edit rules live in `src/scoring/edit.ts` (`withValue`, `withRuleEnabled`, `withPreset` and so on); the store functions in `src/storage/profiles.ts` use the same ones.


## Collapsible league boxes

Every box on the league form (Offense, Offense bonuses, Offense volume, Kicker, IDP, Team defense, Team defense yards allowed, and for imported leagues Stepped rules and Team defense points allowed) is a `Box`: a `fieldset` whose own legend is the toggle, a button with a chevron and `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`. There is no separate summary above it, and a folded box keeps its border and title. The main boxes start open; the optional ones start open only when the league scores something in them. Folding only hides the fields: edits stay in the working copy and Save still writes them.
